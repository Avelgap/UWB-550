We're partnering with companies and brands we trust to offer you even more when you shop on our websites. Here's what that means for you:

Shipping & Returns

  • Items sold and shipped by third-party sellers qualify for free shipping
  • Items may not qualify for all shipping methods offered by Gap Inc.
  • Available shipping methods and prices can be seen at checkout. Some items will require white-glove delivery. If the items qualify, you will be contacted once the item is ready to be scheduled for delivery.
  • Shipped items will ship separately, directly from the seller
  • Items can't be shipped using UPS Access Points

Data Sharing with our Partners

  • Third-party providers only receive your shipping information, including contact information used only to schedule white-glove delivery, if applicable
  • All orders will still be processed securely through our website
  • Items are ineligible for Apple Pay
  • Made-to-order items are ineligible for Afterpay or PayPal
For complete details, see the Banana Republic Shipping Policy, Gap Shipping Policy, Banana Republic Returns Policy and Gap Returns Policy.
